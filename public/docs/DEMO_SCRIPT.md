# Three-minute POLARIS evaluator demonstration
Independent SIH26060 prototype. All synthetic and historical sources stay visibly labelled.

- **0:00–0:25 — The station and its evidence.** Show the Antarctic header, station selector and demonstration label. Open fuel autonomy evidence. Explain that missing operational data remains unavailable.
- **0:25–0:55 — A connected failure.** Open GEN-A in Digital twin. Follow dependencies and inspect the assumed rule, recovery threshold and upstream/downstream paths. Explain that topology is illustrative, not surveyed.
- **0:55–1:30 — Responsibility and action.** In a private session, acknowledge an alert, create/assign a work order, allocate a spare and record work. A completed order does not imply sensor recovery. Prepare a fictional crew record beforehand to show duty assignment.
- **1:30–2:00 — Polar logistics.** Show shipment-linked fuel coverage and the explicit +45-day delay. Under People & operations, toggle the storm assumption on a prepared outdoor task: weather review changes, while the shipment and inventory stay unchanged.
- **2:00–2:30 — Scientific scrutiny.** In Scientific workspace, fail the distribution board to remove the registered backup path. Show thermal assumptions, quality screening and station geography. Avoid claiming real-world predictive accuracy.
- **2:30–3:00 — Handover.** Generate/save a shift report, inspect its evidence and ask how much fuel remains. Show the downloaded snapshot and the browser's real page history.
